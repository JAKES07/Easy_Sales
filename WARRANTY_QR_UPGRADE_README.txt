EASY_SALES WARRANTY + RECEIPT QR UPGRADE

- Owner can set warranty duration in Edit Product.
- Warranty is stored with the product and copied to each sold receipt item.
- Receipt gets a secure random QR token.
- Receipt screen shows the QR at the top-right.
- PDF receipts and Bluetooth ESC/POS receipts print the QR.
- Top-left POS scanner can read receipt QR codes as well as product barcodes.
- QR lookup opens the exact saved receipt and warranty information.
- REFUND and REPLACEMENT actions are available while warranty is active.
- Every action is permanently linked to the original receipt.
- Saved Receipt Documents are updated with status and refund totals.
- Warranty returns are recorded as DAMAGED_RETURN and included in monthly reconciliation.
- Replacement stock is deducted and logged as WARRANTY_REPLACEMENT.
- Cash/card refund values are included in reconciliation cash/card amounts.
- Original sale records are never deleted or overwritten.
